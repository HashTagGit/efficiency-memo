# Bundle third-party pet artwork

EfficiencyMemo bundles two Codex Pet packages whose artwork is not open source: Mole Baby (拉姆 from mole-baby) and Clawd (the pixel crab from clawd-on-desk). Both upstream projects reserve artwork rights — mole-baby leaves 拉姆 art with the 《摩尔庄园》 rights holders, and clawd-on-desk declares its artwork “All Rights Reserved”, non-commercial, with the Clawd character owned by Anthropic — and neither grants redistribution. The local project owner explicitly directed this integration, so the packages ship in the Windows desktop pet catalog with co-located `NOTICE.md` files and an installed `src-tauri/resources/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`, and they are excluded from the MIT license. Public distribution still requires written permission from the rights holders. The decision is reversible: delete the two package directories, their `desktopPet.pet.*` labels, their catalog-test expectations, and the notices.
